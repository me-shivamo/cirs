# Message for the touch-screen rental vendor

Copy and send this to the company supplying the two touch screens for the CIRS booth.

---

Namaste,

For the CIRS booth at the Chinmaya Amrit Mahotsav (Bharat Mandapam), we will show our own videos on the two touch screens you are supplying. Please help us with a few details so everything works on the setup day:

1. **Screen model and size:** what are the make and model of the touch screens?
2. **Built-in system:** is it Android (which version?) or Windows? Or is it only a display that needs a computer connected?
3. **Browser:** is Google Chrome installed, and is the Google Play Store available on it?
4. **Storage:** how much free storage is there? We need about **2 GB** free on each screen.
5. **USB:** does it have a USB port where we can plug in a USB pen drive?
6. **Laptop input:** can we connect a laptop by HDMI, with touch working through a USB cable? If yes, please provide the HDMI cable and the USB touch cable.
7. **Sleep setting:** can the screen's auto sleep / power-saving timer be switched off for the event days?
8. **Internet:** will the screens have Wi-Fi or internet on the setup day? It is needed only once, for about 30 minutes.
9. **Technician:** will a technician be present on the setup day for about 30 minutes, in case we need help installing an app?

Thank you!
CIRS team
