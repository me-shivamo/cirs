*CIRS Touch Screens – Setup at Bharat Mandapam* 🖥️
⏱️ About 20 minutes per screen. Do it on the setup day, one screen at a time.

*Carry:* CIRS pen drive, a phone with hotspot data, the printed Quick Start card and Technician sheet.

*PLAN A – Pen drive (best quality, no internet)*
• Plug the pen drive into the screen (or into a laptop connected to the screen).
• Open *CIRS-KIOSK* → double-click *START-KIOSK*.
• The topics open full screen. ✅ Done in 2 minutes.
👉 Works on Windows screens and laptops. If the screen is Android, or nothing happens, go to Plan B.

*PLAN B – Link (if Plan A does not work)*
1. Open *Chrome* on the screen and go to:
   https://me-shivamo.github.io/cirs/
2. Tap *Download videos* (Wi-Fi or phone hotspot, about 400 MB, 10–15 min). Wait until it says *"All 19 videos are saved"*.
   • No internet? Tap *Load from USB stick* → pen drive → CIRS-KIOSK › videos › hd → ⋮ *Select all* → *Open*.
3. Tap *Add to Home screen* (if shown), then *Start*. ✅
After this it works without internet. Next time, just tap the orange *CIRS Videos* icon.

*Quick check (2 min):* tap 2–3 topics. Each video plays full screen and goes back to the topics by itself.

*If something goes wrong:*
• Screen shows something else → tap the *CIRS Videos* icon.
• Screen frozen → switch it off and on, then tap the icon.
• Touch not working → ask the technician to play *CIRS-Showreel.mp4* from the pen drive on repeat.
• Still stuck after 15 min → hold *"Keep Smiling"* (top right) for 5 seconds, enter PIN *2026*, and send me a photo of that screen.
