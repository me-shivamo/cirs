Touch screen Quick Start

Do these three steps once on each of the two screens, on the setup day. It takes about 20 minutes per screen.

OPEN THIS IN CHROME ON THE SCREEN
cirs-booth.pages.dev

Scan the code with the screen's camera or type the address in Chrome.

1

Tap “Download videos”

The screen needs Wi-Fi for this step only. Wait until it says “All 19 videos are saved”. Keep the screen switched on while it downloads. If the Wi-Fi drops, it carries on by itself.

2

Tap “Add to Home screen”, then “Start”

This puts an orange CIRS Videos icon on the screen's home page. Next time, just tap that icon. No internet is needed after this.

3

Check it

Tap two or three topics. Each video plays full screen and goes back to the topics when it ends. The Home button goes back at any time.

Every morning: switch the screen on and tap the CIRS Videos icon. When nobody touches it for 90 seconds, it plays the videos silently by itself. This is normal.

If something goes wrong

the screen shows something else (a web page, the tablet's home screen)

Tap the orange CIRS Videos icon. If there is no icon, open Chrome. The page opens again.

a video does not play, or the screen is frozen

Switch the screen off and on again, then tap the CIRS Videos icon.

the download is stuck or there is no Wi-Fi

  1. Turn on a phone hotspot and connect the screen to it. The download continues by itself.
  2. Or plug in the USB pen drive, tap “Load from USB stick”, open CIRS-KIOSK › videos › hd, select all 19 files (menu ⋮ › Select all) and tap Open.

the screen has no Chrome, or the steps do not work

Give the Technician sheet and the USB pen drive to the screen company's technician.

touch does not work at all

Ask the technician to play CIRS-Showreel.mp4 from the USB pen drive on repeat. All 19 videos then play one after another.

you are still stuck after 15 minutes

Hold the orange words “Keep Smiling” (top right) for 5 seconds and enter PIN 2026. Take a photo of the screen that opens and send it on WhatsApp to the number below.
